Planet texture attribution
==========================

Real equirectangular surface maps (sun, mercury, venus, earth_day, earth_night,
earth_clouds, moon, mars, jupiter, saturn, saturn_rings, uranus, neptune, ceres,
and the *_texture.jpg aliases derived from them):

  "Solar System Scope" textures — https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
  Distributed under CC Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
  Based on NASA elevation and imagery data. Colors and shades of these
  textures are tuned according to true-color photos made by Messenger,
  Viking, Cassini and New Horizons spacecraft and, in the case of gas
  giants, on Hubble Space Telescope pictures.

Procedurally generated plausible maps (io, europa, ganymede, callisto, titan,
enceladus, triton, pluto, and titan_texture.jpg):

  Generated by scripts/generate-planet-textures.ts in this repository
  (deterministic seeded noise; approximates each body's real gross
  appearance). No third-party imagery. Public domain.
