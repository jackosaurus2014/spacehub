# SpaceNexus / Space Tycoon — Canonical Lore

This document is the shared vocabulary for writers, artists, and engineers working on Space Tycoon. Every new piece of content — contracts, events, dialogue, named NPCs, art briefs — should reference this timeline and these factions for consistency.

**Setting:** The year is **2150 CE.** Humanity has been an active spacefaring civilization for ~90 years. Commercial space operations outpaced national programs by the 2080s. The inner solar system is industrialized; the outer solar system is a frontier; the interstellar era has just begun.

---

## Timeline

### The Commercial Era (2020–2060)
Reusable boosters collapse launch costs. Mega-constellations in LEO bring global broadband. Early lunar and Martian landings are mostly flag-planting. Private companies out-execute national programs but remain dependent on government contracts. **First orbital tourism: 2025. First commercial lunar landing: 2027. First Mars sample return: 2033.**

### The Expansion Era (2060–2089)
Founding of permanent lunar bases at Shackleton (2061) and Malapert (2066). Helium-3 extraction becomes economically viable (2071). First self-sustaining Mars colony at Jezero (2074). Major players: NASA/ESA/JAXA/CNSA plus commercial primes (SpaceX, Blue Origin, CASC-Commercial, Mitsubishi Heavy, Relativity). The regulatory vacuum beyond LEO begins causing conflicts.

### The Accord of Geneva (2089)
After the **Callisto Incident** (2086) — a resource-rights dispute that escalated to seizure of a rival corporation's mining fleet — the UN convenes the Geneva Orbital Summit. The resulting **Accord of Geneva** establishes:

- Corporate charters are recognized as sovereign actors beyond lunar orbit
- Resource extraction rights are tradable property, not national territory
- Contracts between corporations are binding under a new **Spacefaring Commerce Court (SCC)** with arbitration authority
- No military weapons permitted beyond cis-lunar space; defensive systems (shielding, point-defense against debris) allowed
- Annual quarterly reporting required from any corporation with >$1B extraterrestrial assets

This is why Space Tycoon is economic warfare, not kinetic. The Accord is still binding in 2150.

### The Belt Rush (2112–2128)
The discovery of exceptionally high-grade platinum-group concentrations in the asteroid **Psyche-16** triggers a prospecting gold rush. Ceres becomes the logistical hub of the outer inner-system. Corporations that established early belt positions — notably those whose descendants became **The Dominion** — accumulated generational wealth. The Belt Rush is the founding myth of most mid-tier corporations.

### The Consolidation (2128–2143)
The four decades of boom are followed by a wave of mergers and acquisitions. Thousands of small prospecting corps fold into five or six dominant conglomerates. Regulatory authority over beyond-lunar space de facto transfers from national governments to these conglomerates. The first faction alignments crystallize during this period.

### The Fracture (2143)
The **Treaty Fracture of 2143** sees three of the six currently-recognized factions formally withdraw from Accord oversight, creating the modern multi-polar political landscape. The SCC retains binding authority over contracts between Accord-signatory corporations but has no writ over non-signatory faction space. This is the immediate political context of the current era — corporations must navigate which faction spaces their operations touch and which legal frameworks apply.

### The Breakthrough (2147)
**Alcubierre-class metric engines** pass first human interstellar test. The unmanned **Wanderer-1** probe returns telemetry from Proxima Centauri's habitable zone. Crewed interstellar missions are expected within a decade. Every major corporation is positioning for the interstellar era — this is the subject of the **end-game loops** in Space Tycoon.

### The Present (2150)
Players enter the economy here. The solar system is mature but not saturated. Interstellar is imminent but not yet profitable. Corporate warfare is at historic intensity as old powers consolidate and new corporations (the player) race to establish themselves.

## The Six Factions

Each faction has: a core goal, an internal structure, a relationship to the Accord, and a signature operational style.

### The Dominion
**Core goal:** Legitimate sovereignty. Believes the future of humanity in space is a lawful, unified, vertically-integrated empire of commerce.
**Structure:** Hierarchical military-industrial complex. Chartered subsidiaries report upward through regional governors to a board of twelve. Headquarters: **Kepler Station**, GEO above the equator.
**Accord relation:** Signatory and principal enforcer. Accord violators face Dominion patrol intervention.
**Operational style:** Heavy patrol presence in inner-system shipping lanes, punitive customs inspections, high-margin official contracts. Players aligned with the Dominion get priority routing, enforcement escorts, and access to exclusive government-grade contracts.
**Named leaders:** Grand Director **Magnus Varna** (chair), Fleet Admiral **Lyra Chen** (also available as player Commander, Legendary).
**Player rivalry:** Void Corsairs (natural enemy).

### The Syndicate
**Core goal:** Freedom of commerce, above all. Whatever the law won't deliver, the Syndicate will — for a price.
**Structure:** Decentralized network of affiliated families, each controlling a specialty (contraband, intelligence, laundering, gray-market biologicals). No single boss. Headquarters: mobile, but the nominal capital is **Pallas-4 Free Port** in the asteroid belt.
**Accord relation:** Non-signatory. Does not recognize SCC jurisdiction. Syndicate contracts are enforced by reputation, reprisal, and back-channel diplomacy.
**Operational style:** High-margin, high-risk contracts. Discreet logistics. Gray-market goods unavailable through legitimate channels. Players aligned with the Syndicate gain access to black-market prices and information that the Dominion suppresses.
**Named leaders:** **Zahn Eclipse** (Ghost Director — nominal capo, available as Legendary Commander), **Magnate Zara** (the Pallas boss).
**Player rivalry:** The Dominion.

### Void Corsairs
**Core goal:** Independence from all structures. Philosophically opposed to both Dominion order and Syndicate commerce. Raid to survive and to make a point.
**Structure:** Loose confederation of clan-ships, each with its own Warchief. Territorial claims in the outer belt and Saturnian rings. Headquarters: nomadic, clan-by-clan.
**Accord relation:** Treated as pirates by the Accord; consider themselves independent polities.
**Operational style:** Raid convoys, demand tribute, sell passage rights. Pay them off and they look elsewhere. Earn their respect (consistently honor tribute agreements, never hire Dominion patrols against them) and they'll point their raids at your rivals.
**Named leaders:** **Warchief Kraal** (first among equals, available as Legendary Commander), **Rex Ironhide** (former Dominion defector, now second-most-respected Warchief).
**Player rivalry:** The Dominion.

### Hive Collective
**Core goal:** Opaque. The first confirmed non-human intelligence humanity has encountered. Communicates in trade — patterns of goods exchanged — rather than language.
**Structure:** A single distributed consciousness spanning thousands of individual organisms. Nominal "spokesbodies" interface with humans at designated contact stations. Headquarters: the **Great Nest**, a 200km-wide bio-structure in the Kuiper Belt.
**Accord relation:** Observer status. Has not signed. Has not violated.
**Operational style:** Unpredictable; negotiations resemble pattern-matching more than speech. Trades in bio-materials and rare metals no other faction can provide. Aligned players receive exotic resources that unlock endgame research paths.
**Named leaders:** None singular. The Collective's interfaces rotate.
**Player rivalry:** Echo Remnants (mutually incomprehensible).

### Nebula Reavers
**Core goal:** Freedom and drift. Nomadic by philosophy, not poverty. Believe that the only honest life is one untethered to any world.
**Structure:** Fleet-based clans that rendezvous annually at the **Great Convocation** (coordinates change yearly, known only to members). Approximately 40 major ships, hundreds of smaller craft.
**Accord relation:** Signatory in name, non-compliant in practice. The SCC has given up enforcing jurisdiction over them.
**Operational style:** Salvage operators, exotic-fuel brokers, rumor-traders. Map the unmapped; sell the coordinates. Aligned players learn the locations of resource-rich anomalies and short-cut shipping routes.
**Named leaders:** **The Nomad** (the Elder-navigator, available as Legendary Commander, reputed to be 140+ years old on life-extension treatments), **Orion Vex** (war-chief of the largest clan-fleet).
**Player rivalry:** Hive Collective (they distrust the Collective's unreadable motives).

### Echo Remnants
**Core goal:** Preservation. A scholarly enclave that recovers, studies, and protects **precursor artifacts** — traces of a non-human civilization that predated the Hive by millions of years and vanished without explanation.
**Structure:** Guild of orders, each specializing in a category of precursor knowledge (propulsion, computation, energy, materials). Headquarters: **The Archive**, a fortified complex on **Triton**.
**Accord relation:** Signatory. Actively lobbies for stronger regulation of artifact trade.
**Operational style:** Pay premium for precursor finds. Gate access to breakthrough research behind loyalty and trust. Allied players unlock research paths otherwise unreachable. Rival players find their cutting-edge research sabotaged.
**Named leaders:** Archivist **Valeria Starforge** (Grand Master, available as Legendary Commander), **Professor Quark** (theoretician), **Sage** (eldest of the propulsion order).
**Player rivalry:** Hive Collective (the Remnants suspect the Hive know more about the precursors than they admit).

## Named NPCs (non-faction)

### Sovereign Entities
- **The Accord Council** — the governing body of the Geneva Accord, based on Luna. Issues binding rulings via the **Spacefaring Commerce Court (SCC)**. Head: Secretary-General **Anatole Priest**.
- **Belt Miners' Guild** — labor-cooperative representing ~200,000 registered belt miners. Not a faction; negotiates wages and safety standards with all six factions. Speaker: **Iron Mara** (available as Uncommon Commander).
- **The Orbital Engineers' Union** — construction workers and fabricators. Strike-prone and politically powerful.
- **Outer Rim Insurance Mutual** — the largest insurer of outer-system operations; its risk models influence where corporations will operate.

### Cultural Figures
- **Aria Solaris** — celebrity-CEO of the Sunlight Consortium, a solar-power megacorp; frequently a trade partner and rival to players (available as Legendary Commander).
- **Marcus Stellaris** — former Accord Secretary, now senior negotiator for hire (available as Rare Commander).
- **Dr. Vale** — principal author of the Mars Terraforming Protocols; her research unlocks late-game Mars ecology buildings (available as Epic Commander).

### Antagonists (for narrative events, not player targets)
- **The Black Broker** — anonymous information-dealer operating through numbered-account contracts. Has never been seen. Possibly multiple people.
- **Siege Volkov** — former Dominion heavy-ordnance commander, now a mercenary for the highest bidder (available as Uncommon Commander).

## Major Historical Events (event-system fodder)

Writers should reference these when designing in-game events. Events referencing known history reinforce the sense of a lived-in universe.

- **2086 — Callisto Incident** — precipitating event for the Accord.
- **2089 — Accord of Geneva** signed.
- **2097 — Mars Dust Pandemic** — novel respiratory infection killed 12% of Mars surface colonists before a vaccine was developed.
- **2103 — The Great Silence** — 11-month period in which all Hive Collective interface stations went dormant simultaneously, then resumed as if nothing had happened. No explanation was ever provided.
- **2112 — Psyche-16 strike** — start of the Belt Rush.
- **2119 — Europa Subsurface Contact** — failed submarine exploration lost 9 crew; the ocean remains unexplored.
- **2128 — The Kepler Merger Wave** — 74 independent corporations consolidated into 5 in a single year.
- **2137 — The Ring Fire** — Saturn ring-mining accident killed 1,800 workers; led to the modern safety regulations.
- **2143 — The Treaty Fracture** — Void Corsairs, Syndicate, and Hive Collective formally step outside Accord oversight.
- **2147 — Wanderer-1 Return** — first interstellar telemetry; opened the current era.
- **2149 — Triton Archive Breach** — unknown party briefly accessed Echo Remnant archives before being repelled. Ongoing investigation.

## Corporate Naming Conventions

When generating NPC corporations or suggesting names to players:

- **Dominion-aligned:** Imperial suffixes (-Corp, Industries, Systems, Dynamics, Holdings). Example: Varna Dynamics, Kepler Industries, Lyra Systems.
- **Syndicate-aligned:** Old-money family names, opaque abbreviations. Example: Pallas-4 Mercantile, The Morozov Collective, NGA Logistics.
- **Void Corsair clans:** "Clan of X" or "X Host." Example: Clan of the Red Wake, Ironhide Host.
- **Nebula Reaver clans:** Poetic, nature-referencing. Example: Driftline Families, The Long Darkness, Starless Convoy.
- **Echo Remnant guilds:** Academic, archaic. Example: The Order of the First Silence, Triton Archive 7th Chapter.
- **Independent / unaligned:** Founder-name or aspirational. Example: Starforge Ltd, Frontier Prospecting, New Horizon Capital.

## What this document is **not**

- **Not a writ of authority.** Anything here can be changed by the product team. This is the current state of the canon.
- **Not a complete world.** Only what's needed for internal consistency. The Hive Collective's true motivations, the fate of the precursors, the details of interstellar space — all are intentionally left for future content.
- **Not prescriptive of tone.** Individual events and contracts can range from grim to comedic to inspirational; consistency is in the timeline and factions, not in voice.
